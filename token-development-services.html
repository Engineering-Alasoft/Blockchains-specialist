Industry Reality

The Real Reason Tokens Fail to Get Listed

Most token projects treat development and listing as two separate problems. They are not. Exchange listing reviewers look at your contract, your tokenomics, your ownership structure, and your audit trail. If any of these were not built with listing in mind, you go back to square one.

Common rejection reasons we see from projects that come to us after the fact:

01

Ownership Not Renounced Correctly

Ownership not renounced or renounced incorrectly, creating centralization flags that automatically disqualify CEX and launchpad applications.

02

Tokenomics That Signal Dump Risk

Team allocations with no vesting, no lock, no transparency. Reviewers see this immediately and reject before further evaluation.

03

Missing or Incorrect Token Metadata

Missing or incorrect token metadata on-chain and off-chain causes automatic rejection at CMC and CoinGecko submission stage.

04

No Audit Report or Unresolved Critical Issues

No audit report, or an audit that flagged unresolved critical issues. Either outcome blocks listing on any reputable exchange.

05

Tax Mechanics That Break Exchange Routers

Tax mechanics that cause transaction failures on certain exchange smart contract routers — a fatal flaw discovered only at listing attempt.

We have seen every one of these. We build around them from the start.
