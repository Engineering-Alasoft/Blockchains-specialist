How We Work

Our Smart Contract Development Process

Seven disciplined stages from scope to mainnet — no shortcuts, no handoff gaps.

Step 01

Technical Scoping Call

We review your use case, architecture requirements, and security constraints before a single line of code is written. Scope is locked before work begins.

Step 02

Contract Architecture & Design

System design, contract interaction maps, and upgrade strategy defined and documented. You approve the architecture before development starts.

Step 03

Development & Unit Testing

Contracts written in Solidity or Rust depending on chain. Every function is covered by unit tests using Hardhat or Foundry. No untested logic enters review.

Step 04

Internal Security Review

Full internal audit against our security checklist. Identified issues remediated before the contract reaches your team or any external auditor.

Step 05

Testnet Deployment & Client Review

Full testnet deployment with transaction evidence. Client review period before mainnet is approved.

Step 06

Third-Party Audit (If Required)

For DeFi protocols, token launches, and projects handling user funds, we coordinate with CertiK, SlowMist, or OpenZeppelin. We manage the communication and remediation cycle.

Step 07

Mainnet Deployment & Verification

Final deployment to mainnet with Etherscan source verification, ABI delivery, and deployment documentation. Ongoing support available post-launch.
